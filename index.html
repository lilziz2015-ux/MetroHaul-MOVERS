Moving FAQ

Frequently asked moving questions.

Helpful information before requesting your Metro Haul moving quote.

What areas does Metro Haul serve?

Metro Haul serves customers throughout Northern Virginia, Washington DC and nearby Maryland communities. Availability depends on the pickup location, destination and requested move date.

What moving services are available?

Services include residential moving, apartment moving, office moving, loading and unloading, furniture delivery and junk removal.

How do I get a moving estimate?

Start with the online quote form and provide your pickup location, destination, preferred date and basic move information. More details may be requested depending on the job.

Can I hire movers just to load or unload?

Yes. Loading and unloading help is available for rental trucks, trailers, moving containers and storage units.

Can Metro Haul move furniture only?

Furniture pickup and delivery can be requested. Provide the item information, pickup address, destination and any stairs or access details when requesting your quote.
